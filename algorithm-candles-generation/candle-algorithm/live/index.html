        Before you play

        This is a game, not a real market

        • Every market is fictional. Names like RANK SHIFTY, NOGE/BUKT and ROO/BUK are made up. They don't track any real index, currency, metal or coin.
        • Prices are made by a computer model as you watch. They are not quotes and tell you nothing about any real market.
        • Stakes are hamoud, the game's own coins. Hamoud have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold or withdrawn for anything — they are just a score. You are topped up to 1,000 of them each day.
        • The game is built to take the hamoud back. Over a day the house wins back about what the daily top-ups handed out, so the average player ends up where they started. You can watch this happen in the Hamoud economy panel.
        • The game can decide results. The Win rate setting picks each trade's result in advance and steers the chart toward it, so results say nothing about skill or real markets.
        • Everything in the game can be changed or reset at any time.
